01 · MY STORY

From understanding machines
to building intelligent systems.

My engineering journey has never been limited to one discipline. Each experience added another layer to the way I understand systems.

01
THE FOUNDATION

Learning to see engineering as one system

I started my journey in Mechatronics Engineering at Ain Shams University. The field naturally connected the areas that interested me most: mechanical design, electronics, programming, control and automation.

Instead of looking at a machine as separate components, I learned to understand how the mechanical structure, electrical hardware, control logic and software interact.

02
BUILDING

Turning engineering theory into real machines

My university projects pushed that foundation into practical engineering. I worked on robotic systems, automated production lines, machine vision, actuator selection, PID control and system modelling.

This was where engineering became more than equations and drawings. Every design decision had to work together as part of a physical system.

03
THE TURNING POINT

Building a robot that had to actually work

My graduation project, the Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot, brought everything together. I worked with ROS1, LiDAR and EKF-based sensor fusion to implement autonomous navigation and localization.

The system was tested first in simulation and then in the real world, including a custom lifting mechanism and a charging station circuit with BMS monitoring.

A+ Graduation Project From simulation to a physical autonomous system
04
EXPANDING THE TOOLBOX

From physical systems to data and intelligence

I then expanded beyond traditional mechatronics into data analysis, machine learning, deep learning and computer vision.

I worked with Python, SQL, TensorFlow, OpenCV and Scikit-learn, building practical AI/ML projects and learning how data can become another engineering tool for understanding and improving systems.

05
ENGINEERING IN THE REAL WORLD

Working with operations, machines and people

Professional experience added another dimension. At Delotrans, I worked with large-scale logistics operations, compliance and technical coordination.

Today, as a Technical Office Engineer at Orascom Services, I work with Volvo Penta engine operations, spare-parts identification, technical requirements and documentation.

These experiences taught me that engineering is not only about building technology. It is also about understanding requirements, communicating clearly, solving problems and making systems work reliably in real operations.

06
WHERE I AM GOING

Build. Test. Analyze. Improve.

Today, my engineering interests span robotics, automation, embedded systems, software testing, data and intelligent systems.

The common thread is simple: I enjoy understanding complex systems, getting them to work, finding what can be improved and turning ideas into practical solutions.

The next system is always another opportunity to learn, build and improve.

03 · SELECTED WORK

Three projects.
Three engineering challenges.

From renewable energy to industrial automation and autonomous robotics, each project challenged me to connect multiple engineering disciplines.

01
GRADUATION PROJECT · ROBOTICS

Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot

Building an autonomous robot capable of navigating and performing shelf-lifting operations.

The Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot was my graduation project and the point where multiple areas of mechatronics came together.

I implemented autonomous navigation and localization using ROS1, LiDAR and EKF-based sensor fusion. The system was validated through both Gazebo simulation and real-world testing.

The project also included a custom lifting mechanism and a charging station circuit with BMS monitoring.

ROS1 LiDAR EKF Sensor Fusion Gazebo Embedded Systems
A+ Grade Graduation Project

02
RENEWABLE ENERGY · DESIGN

Water Turbine

Designing an undershot water turbine and pushing engineering performance through competition.

This project challenged us to transform mechanical design principles into a working energy-generation system.

I led the team through the design challenge, developing an undershot water turbine that achieved approximately 60% efficiency.

The project competed against 42 teams in a competition sponsored by the Center of Excellence of Energy and Arizona State University.

Mechanical Design Renewable Energy Fluid Systems Engineering Analysis
1st Place Among 42 teams
▶ Watch Demo
03
INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION · ROBOTICS

Fully Automated Production Line

Designing a production line with dual robotic arms and machine vision.

I designed a complete production-line concept containing Feeding, Sorting, Assembly and Packaging stations.

The system used two different robotic mechanisms: a revolute arm for sorting and a Cartesian arm for assembly operations.

I applied PID control for precise positioning at the packaging station and used MATLAB to determine actuator sizes and select suitable actuators.
