01 · MY STORY

From understanding machines to building systems.

My journey started with a simple curiosity: how do things actually work?

That question led me into Mechatronics Engineering, where mechanical systems, electronics, programming, control and automation became parts of one bigger picture.

Over time, that picture grew beyond machines. I became interested in robotics, software, data and intelligent systems — and in how all of them can work together to solve real problems.

01

THE FOUNDATION

Learning how systems work

I studied Mechatronics Engineering at Ain Shams University, developing a foundation across mechanical and electrical engineering, programming, control systems and automation.

Instead of seeing these as separate subjects, I learned to see them as connected pieces of the same engineering system.

02

BUILDING

Turning ideas into machines

University projects gave me the opportunity to move from theory into physical systems.

I worked with automated production lines, robotic arms, machine vision, PLCs, PID control, SolidWorks, MATLAB and Siemens TIA Portal.

This was where engineering stopped being something I only studied — and became something I could build.

03

THE TURNING POINT

Building an autonomous robot

My graduation project brought many of these disciplines together.

I developed an Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot designed to navigate and perform shelf-lifting operations autonomously.

The project involved ROS1, LiDAR, EKF-based sensor fusion, autonomous navigation, embedded systems and Gazebo, with validation through real-world testing.

A+ Graduation Project
04

EXPANDING

From hardware to software

Working with engineering systems made me increasingly interested in the software behind them.

I started developing skills in software testing, Java, APIs, Jira, Postman, Selenium and ISTQB-based testing practices.

I became interested not only in how software is built, but also in how it behaves, how it fails and how it can be improved.

05

DATA & AI

Learning to work with data

I also expanded my technical background into Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

During my AI/ML internship at Elevvo Pathways, I worked with Python, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, TensorFlow, OpenCV and Scikit-learn.

This gave me another way to understand engineering problems — through both physical systems and data.

06

REAL-WORLD ENGINEERING

Working with real operations

My professional experience has taken me beyond engineering projects and into real operational environments.

At Delotrans, I developed experience in operations, coordination, compliance and problem-solving while managing work related to 255 trucks and supporting GTG/PTI processing for more than 8,800 drivers.

Today, at Orascom Services, I work as a Technical Office Engineer supporting Volvo Penta engine operations through spare-parts identification, technical documentation and requirements.

07

TODAY

Still building. Still learning.

Today, my interests sit at the intersection of engineering and technology — robotics, automation, embedded systems, software testing, data and intelligent systems.

I continue to develop across these areas because I believe the strongest solutions come from understanding the complete system, not just one component.
