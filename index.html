01 · MY STORY

From understanding machines
to engineering complete systems.

My engineering journey has never been limited to one discipline. I started with mechatronics and gradually moved through mechanical systems, electronics, automation, robotics, embedded software, verification, software testing and data.

Each stage added another layer to the way I understand engineering: not only how to build something, but how the parts interact, how the system behaves, how it can fail and how it can be improved.

01
THE FOUNDATION

Mechatronics gave me the systems mindset.

I studied Mechatronics Engineering at Ain Shams University. The discipline gave me a foundation across mechanical design, electronics, programming, control, automation and embedded systems.

Instead of looking at engineering problems as isolated subjects, I learned to see the relationship between the physical system, electronics, software and control.

02
BUILDING

I started turning engineering concepts into systems.

During university, I worked on robotics, automated production systems, machine vision, control and mechanical design.

I used tools including SolidWorks and MATLAB and worked with robotic systems, sensors, actuators, PID control and automation concepts.

Separately, I developed my industrial automation knowledge through PLC programming, Siemens TIA Portal, Ladder Logic, HMI and production-line simulation.

03
GRADUATION PROJECT · A+

I built an autonomous warehouse robot.

My Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot project brought mechanical, electrical and software engineering together in one complete system.

The robot used ROS1, LiDAR and EKF-based sensor fusion for autonomous navigation and localization. I also worked with Gazebo, embedded systems, the physical robot, its lift mechanism and charging/power systems.

The project was completed with an A+ grade.

A+ Autonomous Shelf Lift Robot
04
EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

I learned to work closer to the hardware.

I developed embedded systems using microcontrollers, sensors, communication protocols and peripheral devices.

One example was an ATmega32 attendance system using an AS608 fingerprint sensor over UART, DS1307 RTC, EEPROM, keypad, LCD and buzzer.

Later, I worked on ESP32-C3 based IoT systems involving temperature, humidity and water-level monitoring, ESP-NOW communication and MQTT-based backend integration.

05
DIGITAL VERIFICATION

I moved from building systems to verifying them.

My Digital Verification project focused on verifying a PCIe 5.0 Transaction Layer using SystemVerilog and UVM.

This introduced me to another side of engineering: understanding requirements, designing verification environments, creating test scenarios, analyzing failures and making sure a complex system behaves according to its specification.

SystemVerilog UVM PCIe 5.0 Verification
06
SOFTWARE TESTING

The testing mindset became a professional direction.

I became increasingly interested in the software side of engineering — especially how software behaves, how defects are discovered and how quality can be verified systematically.

Through my DEPI Software Testing track, I have been developing practical knowledge in ISTQB fundamentals, static testing, test analysis and design, Jira, Postman, Selenium, Java and Agile testing.

My engineering background gives me a useful perspective here: I naturally think about requirements, system behavior, edge cases, failures and root causes.

07
DATA & INTELLIGENT SYSTEMS

I expanded from systems to the data behind them.

I also developed skills in data analysis and artificial intelligence, working with Python, SQL, NumPy, Pandas and machine-learning tools.

This helped me look at engineering systems from another perspective: not only how they operate, but what their data can tell us about their performance and behavior.

08
TODAY

Engineering beyond the classroom.

My professional experience has added another dimension to my engineering approach.

At Delotrans, I worked with logistics operations, compliance and technical systems. Today at Orascom Services, I work in Technical Office and Spare Parts Operations, dealing with technical documentation, parts identification, requirements and coordination.

I am continuing to build across robotics, automation, embedded systems, software testing, data and intelligent systems because I want to understand engineering as a complete process.
